By the numbers
United States · 2025
745,652 people experienced homelessness on a single night in January 2025.
266,320 people were experiencing unsheltered homelessness in January 2025.
-3.3% change in overall homelessness compared with the 2024 count.
+6.8% increase in homelessness among adults age 65 and older.
